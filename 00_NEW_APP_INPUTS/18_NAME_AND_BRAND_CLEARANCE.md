# Thikana — Name and Brand Clearance

## Owner's selected working name

**Thikana**

## Research finding

The name is already used by multiple Bangladesh property-related products:
1. Google Play app named "Thikana" by Jalali Tech Store (`com.doracone.thikana.app`) advertises flat, land and shop sale/rental.
2. Google Play app "Thikana ঠিকানা (জমি কেনা বেচা)" by kobul.com (`bd.thikana.android`) describes a property marketplace and lists rent as a recent feature.
3. `thikanakujun.com` operates a Thikana property marketplace site.
4. `thikana.bd` is associated with the other Thikana app.
5. `thikana.top` describes property management software for Bangladesh.

These are exact or near-exact overlaps in the same country and property market.

## Risk

- Customers may confuse the apps.
- Search results may be dominated by existing products.
- App-store reviewers/users may mistake one product for another.
- Domain and social handle availability may be limited.
- A registered mark may exist in relevant service classes.
- The existence of apps does not conclusively determine trademark ownership or whether a new mark can legally be registered.

## Recommendation

Keep "Thikana" only as an internal working title until a proper clearance check. Do not finalize:
- app icon/logo;
- public website/domain;
- Android applicationId / iOS bundle identifier;
- company name;
- paid advertising;
- social media account names;
- public store listing.

## Required clearance

1. Search Bangladesh DPDT official trademark register for exact and similar marks.
2. Search relevant classes and descriptions for property listing, rental intermediary, real estate advertising, marketplace software and related services. Ask trademark counsel which classes apply.
3. Check company/business name availability separately.
4. Search Google Play and Apple App Store for exact, similar and transliterated names.
5. Check .com, .com.bd, .bd and suitable domains; registrar records may differ from a simple browser search.
6. Check Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and other brand handles.
7. Consult a Bangladesh trademark lawyer before filing.
8. Record date, query and result evidence in this file.

## Owner decision

- [ ] Keep Thikana only if cleared.
- [ ] Choose a distinctive new name before public branding.
- [ ] Ask for a shortlist of more distinctive alternatives.

No trademark clearance is claimed by this research.
