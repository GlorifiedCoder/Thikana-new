## Research questions for future validation

- In which Dhaka neighbourhoods do renters struggle most to find current listings?
- Do renters prefer direct phone/WhatsApp, viewing requests, or in-app messaging?
- What verification evidence will landlords actually provide?
- What percentage of listers are owners vs agents?
- Which property categories produce the most repeat searches?
- Are renters willing to report inaccurate listings and what evidence can they provide?
- Will owners reconfirm availability every 14–30 days?
- Which channels can provide legitimate initial listing supply?
- What is the best approach to local map data, area aliases, and Bangla transliteration?
- What payment/monetization model is acceptable after trust is established?
- Can a legally compliant production database be hosted in the selected Supabase region?

## Main research conclusion

The general rental listing features are already offered by multiple competitors. The product opportunity is an operationally credible trust and freshness system, clear cost details, owner-authorized listings, and fast, localized search—not merely reproducing a classifieds feed.
