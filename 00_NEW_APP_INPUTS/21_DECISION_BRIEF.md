# Thikana — Decision Brief for Owner

Please review these decisions before ZCode begins implementation.

## A. Brand

Research found existing Thikana apps and websites in the same property sector. Do you want to keep it as a temporary codename while exploring a more distinctive public name?

## B. Launch area

Recommended: Dhaka pilot first, with nationwide data structure. Select a few neighbourhood clusters only after supply research.

## C. First release categories

Recommended first public release:
- Flat/apartment and full house.
- Room, seat/bed and sublet.
- Shop and office.

Hostel/mess and warehouse/godown can be included if the listing model is ready; do not build every possible real estate category at once.

## D. Contact

Recommended: call/WhatsApp where lister opts in, plus structured viewing request. Defer full in-app chat until moderation and abuse operations are ready.

## E. Verification

Recommended:
- Phone OTP for listers.
- Owner/agent declaration.
- Manual review for new/high-risk listings.
- Availability reconfirmation date.
- Do not claim ownership verified without evidence.
- Do not collect NID by default.

## F. Legal issues

Before public release, lawyer to review:
- House Rent Control Act treatment of deposit/advance fields.
- Data Protection Act 2026 and Supabase cross-border hosting.
- Consumer/digital-commerce obligations.
- Thikana brand trademark/name.
- Terms, privacy policy and listing intermediary role.

## G. Monetization

Recommended: free browse, post and contact at launch. Consider clearly labelled paid promotion later. No pay-to-unlock phone credits and no rent/deposit collection in MVP.

## H. Design

Use user's supplied design references (copy them into assets/screenshots). Support System, Light Blue, Light Gold and Dark theme options. Bangla and English.
