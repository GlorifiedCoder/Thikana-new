## Competitor evidence and caveats

### Tolet BD
Its public website describes family houses, bachelor rooms, hostel seats, sublets, offices and shops; location selection by division, district, thana and subarea; listing and direct contact workflows.

### THE TOLET
Its FAQ describes owner listing submission and review, search by location and property details, and credit-based contact information. Google Play review examples complain about rented/old listings and contact failures. Individual reviews are anecdotal, not a statistical quality measurement.

### Dhaka Resident
The public website/help centre describes review of each listing before publication, approximate public location, enquiries and viewing requests. This is a useful benchmark feature, but performance and scale are not established.

### Name collision
The Google Play app named "Thikana" by Jalali Tech Store describes the same property rental/sales category and lists the domain `thikanakujun.com`. Another app `bd.thikana.android` uses "Thikana ঠিকানা" and `thikana.bd`. The name is commercially crowded in the target market.

## What Thikana should not copy blindly

- Paid credits to reveal basic owner contact before listing accuracy is reliable.
- Generic "verified" badges without evidence definition.
- Nationwide advertising before local supply exists.
- Unmoderated user-submitted listings.
- Full exact address/phone disclosure by default.
- Land sales, home sales, rent management, payments, social feed and unrelated categories in the first release.

## Where to differentiate

- Freshness: reconfirmed date and automated expiry.
- Evidence-specific badges.
- Transparent total recurring cost and explicit caveats about upfront payments.
- Better listing quality and active owner response.
- Simple listing wizard and immediate rented/unavailable status.
- Moderation and complaint resolution metrics.
