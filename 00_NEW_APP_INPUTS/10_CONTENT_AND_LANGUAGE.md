# Content, Language and Localization — Thikana

## App languages

- Bangla and English.
- Default Bangla recommended for Bangladesh launch, with clear language switch.
- All user-generated listing titles/descriptions may be Bangla, English or mixed.
- Search should handle Bangla script, English spelling, common transliteration, aliases and neighbourhood spellings.
- Do not machine-translate property/legal claims without marking them as translations.

## Currency, dates and units

- Currency: Bangladeshi Taka (৳ / BDT).
- Rent basis: monthly by default.
- Date/time display: Bangladesh locale and Asia/Dhaka timezone.
- Area: square feet for MVP; support user-entered optional local measurements only after clear conversion rules.
- Never convert or round rent/area in a way that changes advertiser's stated terms.

## Listing content types

- Text description.
- Property photos.
- Map/approximate location.
- Amenities and facilities.
- Rent and recurring cost details.
- Availability date/status.
- Owner/agent profile and evidence-specific verification labels.
- Optional viewing enquiry.

## Content style

- Clear, practical, trustworthy.
- Do not use misleading claims such as "100% verified", "guaranteed safe", "best price" or "available now" without evidence.
- Display "Advertiser-provided information" where not independently checked.
- Explain verification badge meaning inline.
- Make "last availability confirmed" easy to find.
- Safety advice should be direct and non-alarmist.

## Accessibility

- Support larger font sizes, screen readers, high contrast, visible keyboard focus on admin web, touch targets and reduced motion.
- Bengali fonts must render cleanly on lower-end Android devices.
- Do not rely on color alone for status/verifications.
