# Thikana — Source Register

Research access date: 20 November 2026. Public webpages may change; recheck before implementation decisions and store submission.

## Bangladesh laws and official sources

**S1 — Personal Data Protection Act, 2026, Laws of Bangladesh**
- https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-details-1692.html?lang=en
- Official search result/text: https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1692.html
- Act No. 63 of 2026, dated 10 April 2026.
- Use for consent/lawful basis, data subject rights, confidentiality, safeguards, retention, records, data transfer and deferred commencement review.

**S2 — Personal Data Protection Act, 2026, data security section**
- https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1692/section-print-57283.html
- Section 17 text includes technical and organizational safeguards, encryption/pseudonymisation and periodic assessment/testing.

**S3 — Personal Data Protection Act, 2026, cross-border and retention overview**
- https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1692.html
- See sections 18, 19 and 29 in the official act text.

**S4 — House Rent Control Act, 1991**
- https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-748.html
- Official Bengali text; section 2 definition, section 10 premium/salami/security and advance restrictions, section 13 rent receipt, section 14 refund of prohibited sums, section 15 standard rent.
- Legal interpretation should be confirmed by Bangladesh counsel.

**S5 — Transfer of Property Act, 1882, section 107**
- https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-48/section-print-16322.html
- Lease from year to year or more than one year or reserving yearly rent requires registered instrument, subject to the Act and exceptions.

**S6 — Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines, 2021, Bangladesh Government Press**
- https://www.dpp.gov.bd/bgpress/index.php/document/get_extraordinary/40432
- Official Gazette page shows 4 July 2021 Extraordinary Gazette entry by Ministry of Commerce.

**S7 — Bangladesh DPDT**
- https://dpdt.portal.gov.bd/
- https://dpdtbd.com/search
- Use official trademark search and obtain professional advice; this research has not completed a formal clearance.

## Research caveats

- Public store download counts are buckets and not market share or active user counts.
- Competitor pages describe their own products and may be incomplete or stale.
- User reviews are anecdotes, not representative surveys.
- No independent audit of competitor verification methods was performed.
- No legal opinion was obtained.
- No formal DPDT trademark clearance was performed.
- No API provider pricing contract or hosting region was selected.
